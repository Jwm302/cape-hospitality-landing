# LinkedIn posting guide

For the co-founder who posts for Cape Hospitality Advisors. One page to keep the posts consistent, useful and safe.

## Who we talk to
Product, contracting and quality managers at German and European tour operators that sell Cape Town and the Garden Route, plus Cape Town DMCs. They care about one thing: will their guests get what was sold, and will there be complaints?

## Voice
- First person, from the field: "This morning I timed the shower in a heritage-wing room: 4 minutes to warm up."
- Facts over adjectives: times, temperatures, decibels, counts.
- Calm and fair to hotels. We report what we find; we never mock or shame.
- Short: 80–200 words, short paragraphs, one idea per post.
- English on the personal profile; German versions on the company page (or in the post, after the English, for key posts).

## Post types (rotate)
1. **What we found** – an anonymised finding from an inspection and what it means for guests. Weekly anchor.
2. **Data note** – one number from guest reviews, e.g. "Hot water is the #2 complaint from German guests in Cape Town hotels this year."
3. **Behind the scenes** – a photo or 20-second video from an inspection day (no hotel names, no recognisable people).
4. **How to** – a practical tip for product managers, e.g. "3 clauses to add before signing a hotel allocation."
5. **Invitation** – every 3–4 posts: "Send me a hotel from your Cape Town programme and I'll run the free check."

Call to action (when there is one): **"Check any hotel in your Cape Town programme — free"** with the link `https://www.capehospitalityadvisors.com/#virtual-audit` (German: `https://www.capehospitalityadvisors.com/?lang=de#virtual-audit`). Add UTM tags once tracking is set up.

## Weekly routine (about 20–30 minutes)
1. **Monday:** send Claude the raw material – photos, voice notes or bullet points from the week, plus any idea.
2. **Claude** drafts 2 posts (plus German versions and cropped images) into the scheduling tool (Taplio or Typefully).
3. **You** read each draft, add one personal detail, approve. Team members can comment on drafts.
4. **Posting days:** Tuesday and Thursday mornings, 08:00–09:00 German time.
5. **First hour after posting:** reply to every comment yourself. The other two founders like, comment or reshare.
6. **Monthly:** Claude summarises which posts led to profile visits, comments and enquiries.

## Do
- Reply personally to comments and messages; that is where enquiries start.
- Connect with relevant people with a short personal note, a few a day.
- Use real photos from inspections, edited only for light and cropping.
- Label illustrations or examples as such (e.g. "Example from our sample report").

## Don't
- Name a client, an audited hotel or a guest; show recognisable faces without written permission.
- Post anything a hotel could recognise as its own property without the client's agreement.
- Use bots or tools that send automatic connection requests, messages or comments (LinkedIn restricts such accounts).
- Send cold marketing emails to German contacts (§7 UWG); move to email only after they reply or ask.
- Claim results we can't show ("hotels improve 40% after our audit").

## Access
- Company page: all three founders are admins (page **Settings → Manage admins**).
- Scheduling tool: one team workspace; each person posts from their own LinkedIn account.
- Each founder lists Cape Hospitality Advisors under **Experience** and links the website in their profile.
